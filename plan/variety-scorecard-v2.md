## Freezing rules
- Gates marked **power check** are frozen only after a planted-effect simulation shows what they detect at 80% power (L5, S6). The draft number stays as written unless the simulation shows the gate can't detect the effect it claims. A change then goes back to steering before freezing, never after grading.
- A test that can't run (missing data, no speech yet, no client yet) is **inconclusive**, which is not a pass.
- Every test reports its effect size and n, not just pass/fail.
- Overall v2 verdict: pass only if v1 tests 1–6 and v2 tests 7–15 all pass. Test 16 is reported separately as the **naturalness verdict**, because it needs people and can't run every time.

The stub must fail (amendment 2) | true accuracy ~0.45 (0.40: 54%, 0.45: 87%; chance 0.20 passes ~0) | The soul also names age, role, wake time and sleep need (memory.py:41–42), all code-driven, so they would let a judge match on circumstance. Twice chance is the minimum at which a player could tell people apart. **Day-log (clarification, 2026-11-05):** one agent's waking day from the event log, as time-ordered lines: activity and place changes, talks (with whom), every receipt (action and outcome), meals, gifts and thefts, each deliberation's action and reason, and the reflection summary. Core entries are left out: since engine 4 they record who did what and where, and those events are already in the day-log as talk and receipt lines. Before the judge sees it, the day-log is masked by word stem, uniformly for every agent, so the mask itself is no clue. Take every content word of every trait-level phrase and every value name `render_soul` can produce (stop words like "to", "your" and "with" left out). Use its first 5 letters (or the whole word if shorter) as a stem, and add a frozen synonym list: indep, routin, famil, caref, solitu, novel, spont, grudg, resent, brave, fearl, anxie, curios, stimul. Any day-log word that starts with a stem of 4 or more letters is replaced whole with "[…]", and stems under 4 letters ("new", "set") are masked as whole words only. Numbers that quote a meter ("hunger 866/1000", "affinity -400") are masked the same way. Other agents' names stay. **Precondition (otherwise test 11 is inconclusive):** on the masked day-logs, a held-out cue list must find own-sheet cues in at most 0.01 more texts than donor-sheet cues (a fixed donor, the next agent id). The review thread writes that list, it is frozen by sha256 before grading, and whoever builds the mask never sees it. Without the mask, a judge could match on parroting: 4B deliberation reasons name the agent's own sheet phrases in 41% of cases at engine 5 (character-sheet quoting, below). An exact-phrase mask alone still left own-sheet cues in 12.4% of t1i-4b texts (review check, reviews/pr17-mask-leak-check.py). The stem mask without the synonym list leaves 0.9% to 2.0% more own than donor cues on t1e-4b, t1i-4b and t1i-8b (results/derived/stem_mask_check.py), and the synonyms come from that check's cue list, which is why the precondition needs a list nobody tuned the mask on |

## Diagnostics (reported every run, no gate)
Cheap proxies for test 16's tells, so obvious ones get caught without people:
- Meter quoting: the share of mind outputs that quote a numeric need or affinity ("hunger 866/1000", "affinity -400"; the relationships file prints affinity, memory.py:59).
- Character-sheet quoting: the share of mind texts (deliberation reasons, reflection summaries, core entries) that contain one of the author's own sheet phrases: the trait-level phrases of its "You are …" line and its top and bottom value names. It's reported next to the same share for a fixed donor's phrases (the next agent id, with phrases the author shares removed) as the shared-vocabulary baseline. Values on full arms so far: 4B 0.685, 0.316 and 0.252 at engines 3, 4 and 5 (at engine 5, reasons 0.407, summaries 0.119, cores 0); 8B 0.148, 0.009 and 0.009; donor baselines at most 0.011; no_text arms at most 0.001. This exact-phrase share undercounts, because it misses inflections and paraphrases. Test 11's day-log uses the stem mask above, and test 16's tells catalog checks with the stem version.
- Futile repeats: the same failed action at the same place 3+ times with no new information.
- Behavior mix: socializing hours per agent-day, transgressions per agent-week by honesty quartile.
- The current values for these come from the existing logs, and the report shows them from the next update on.

Mind authorship (test 7's companion, from `python -m asiimov.authorship`, a replay instrument that changes no rule or log):
- **Divergent mind share:** per agent, the share of waking ticks where a mind-authored activity (plan block or deliberated override) differs from what the habit layer picks at that tick (`Sim._habit` on `_act`'s 6-tick cadence). Whole-day plans make test 7's raw share near 1 even when they copy the habits; this says how much of the day the mind actually changed.
- **Effective mind share:** the same, counting only changes in place or in work-or-not, the only parts of an activity the rules read ("rest" and "home" differ in name only).
- Reported as median, mean and share of agents below 0.20, next to test 7's raw numbers, with the plan-vs-habit activity pairs. No gate: the harness v2 decision rule (shared folder plan/mind-harness-v2.md) reads divergent share; test 7's gate stays as frozen.
- Caveat: the habit pick is counterfactual within the world the mind made, not a twin run, so it measures override of the habit layer, not a different life.
